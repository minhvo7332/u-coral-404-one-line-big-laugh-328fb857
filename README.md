## The line

> My horoscope said I'd meet my soulmate today, so I stayed home. I don't want to meet me.

Why this line: the setup promises a romantic encounter, then the turn inverts
it — the narrator stays home to *avoid* the soulmate, because the soulmate is
them. The reason is felt in the act (staying home), never stated. Short,
fresh angle, no stock pun, reads smoothly aloud in one breath.
